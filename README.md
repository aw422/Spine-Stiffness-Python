Lumbar Spine Stiffness Analysis Pipeline

This project aimed to quantify a persons in vivo through range axial rotation lumbar spine stiffness. 
This was achieved by securly fixing the person's torso (above L1) and having them stand on a rotating platform that was then rotated at a fixed rate. 
Inertial measurement units were placed on the person's T12, S1 and on the handle of the winch used to turn the rotating platform at a constant rate. 
The force required to rotate the platform was measures as was the change in Yaw of the lumbar and sacrum IMUs 
and with this the range or movement and then stiffness of the lumbar spine in axial rotation could be calculated.
